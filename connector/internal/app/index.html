Connect to Tally

Looking for Tally…

  1. Open TallyPrime and load your company.
  2. Press F1 (Help) → Settings → Connectivity. In Tally.ERP 9: F12 → Advanced Configuration.
  3. Set TallyPrime acts as to Both (or Server) and Port to 9000. Accept and restart Tally if asked.

This page checks again automatically every few seconds.
