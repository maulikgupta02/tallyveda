Share Tally data with your bank

This tool reads your accounts from Tally on this computer and sends them securely to your bank. It does not change anything in Tally.

✓Companies shared from this computer
1Connect to Tally
Looking for Tally…
  1. Open TallyPrime and load your company.
  2. Press F1 (Help) → Settings → Connectivity. In Tally.ERP 9: F12 → Advanced Configuration.
  3. Set TallyPrime acts as to Both (or Server) and Port to 9000. Accept and restart Tally if asked.

This page checks again automatically every few seconds.

2Enter the code from your bank
3Choose the company and approve
4Sending
Waiting…

Tally stays usable while this runs: the connector reads a small piece at a time and pauses in between. You can keep working in Tally, or close this tab and the sync carries on in the background.

Done. Your bank has received the data.
