This notice explains how {{ operator }} ("we") handles information when a business shares its accounting data through Tally Connector, and the rights you have. We process this data only with your consent, given in Tally Connector before anything is shared.

1. Who we are

{{ operator }}{% if address %}, {{ address }}{% endif %}, operates Tally Connector, which reads a business's books of account from Tally on its own computer and shares them with the organisation that requested them, for credit assessment.

2. What we collect

  • Books of account for the period you approve: account groups, ledgers (including the names, GSTINs and states of customers and suppliers recorded in them), vouchers, outstanding bills and stock values.
  • Consent record: the name of the person giving consent, the email address verified with a one-time code, the date and time, the network (IP) address and the exact consent wording.
  • Technical diagnostics: the Tally Connector version, the Tally version and add-on settings, computer memory and connection logs, used only to keep the service working and to resolve problems.

Tally Connector only reads data. It never changes anything in Tally.

3. Why we use it

  • To share your books with the organisation that requested them, and with the banks and financial institutions it works with, solely to assess and process your credit application.
  • If you choose daily updates, to keep that assessment current until you or the organisation stop them.
  • To give your business its own dashboard of the same analysis.
  • To operate, secure and support the service.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

4. Who we share it with

  • The organisation that sent you the access code, and the banks and financial institutions it works with, for the purpose above.
  • Service providers that host and run the service for us (cloud hosting, database and email), under confidentiality and security obligations. Your data is currently stored on their servers in {{ data_location }}.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

  • Raw books are deleted {{ retention_days }} days after sharing ends (daily updates off or stopped and no new data received).
  • The figures of each completed assessment are kept as the record of that assessment, until a deletion request is carried out or the law requires otherwise.
  • Copies of the raw data behind older reports are kept for {{ keep_raw_days }} days, plus one per month.

6. How we protect it

Data is encrypted in transit, access is limited to authorised accounts with passwords stored only in hashed form, administrative actions are logged, and each organisation sees only the businesses it requested.

7. Your rights

  • Withdraw consent to further sharing at any time: select "Stop daily updates" in Tally Connector or on your dashboard. Withdrawal does not affect sharing that already took place.
  • Access, correction and erasure: see what we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. You can request deletion from your dashboard or by writing to us.
  • Grievances: we respond to requests and grievances within 90 days at most.
  • Nomination: you may nominate a person to exercise these rights on your behalf.
  • Complaint: if you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
