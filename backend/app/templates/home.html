How it works

From request to analysis in one sitting.

No document chase, no spreadsheet exports, no accountant in the loop.

01

The bank sends a code

Create a request on the dashboard and share the one-time code with the borrower, by WhatsApp or email.

02

The MSME runs the connector

One file, double-clicked on the PC where Tally is open. It reads 24 months of books, asks for consent and sends them over HTTPS. Nothing in Tally changes.

03

Analysis, refreshed daily

Sales, margins, receivables, cash cycle, debt service, banking and tax, rated against your policy, with alerts the morning anything moves.

Red-flag checks

The things an audit finds a year late, on day one.

Stuck receivables

Customers who have stopped paying, named, with what they owe.

Books not up to date

How many days behind the last entry is, so you know what you are looking at.

Large cash receipts and payments

Cash receipts of ₹2 lakh or more (s.269ST) and cash payments over ₹10,000 (s.40A(3)).

Circular trading

Parties that appear as both customer and supplier with offsetting flows.

Backdated or deleted entries

Gaps and out-of-order voucher numbering that suggest the books were edited after the fact.

Cash going negative

Days the cash book dipped below zero, a classic sign of unrecorded receipts.
